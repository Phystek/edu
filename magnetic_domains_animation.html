Inside the Core: Magnetic Domains and the B–H Loop

ELEC2005 · Week 10

Why does iron carry so much more flux than air, why does it stop at a limit, and why does a core get warm on AC? The answers are all inside the material. Step through the six short chapters to watch the domains respond to the coil current while the B–H curve draws itself. Then take control of the field yourself.

A cartoon, not a micrograph. Each grain is drawn as a single domain that flips as a whole, and the field axis has no units. In real iron the domains are far smaller and grow by moving their walls, and a transformer steel's loop is much narrower than the one drawn here. The shapes and the order of events are right; the sizes are exaggerated.
