From Current to Flux: Wire, Coil and Core

ELEC2005 · Week 10

Every magnetic device starts with a current. Follow one wire as it becomes a loop, then a coil, then a coil on an iron core. The compass needles show the direction of the field intensity H, and the flowing dots show where the flux goes: the longer and faster they are, the stronger the flux density B.

Seen end-on, and partly drawn by hand. We look along the wires, which are long and straight into the screen, so the needle directions are calculated exactly for that case. The needles also feel a weak background field so that they point up, like a compass pointing north, when no current flows. The iron is drawn schematically: the rod uses an effective permeability of 40 and the closed core a relative permeability of 1000.
