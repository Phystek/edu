Ideal vs Actual Transformer

ELEC2005 · Week 10

An ideal transformer needs no magnetising current. A real one does, and how that current behaves depends on how you drive it. The magnetising current is the running total of the applied voltage (v1 = LM diM/dt). Switch between a DC and an AC source to see why a transformer only works on AC.

Simplified model. LM is treated as constant, and the core is taken to saturate when the magnetising current reaches a fixed 4 A. A real core saturates gradually, at a level set by its material and size, and the current that follows is limited only by the winding resistance.
