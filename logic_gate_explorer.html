Both gates use the same two diodes and the same idea — only the diode direction and which rail the resistor pulls toward differ. The OR gate's diodes point toward the output and its resistor pulls down to 0 V; the AND gate's diodes point toward the inputs and its resistor pulls up to 5 V. Adjust the inputs and watch which diode actually conducts.
