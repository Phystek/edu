Transformer Action: Flux, Turns and Power

ELEC2005 · Week 10

Two windings on one core, with no wire joining them, and yet power gets from one to the other. Watch the flux swing round the core, see each turn of wire pick up the same voltage, and find out why the primary current rises when you connect a load.

Slowed right down, with very few turns. The AC here takes 4 seconds per cycle; on the mains it repeats 200 times faster. A real winding has hundreds of turns, not a handful. The transformer is ideal apart from a small magnetising current, and values are peaks: 12 V on the primary and a 2 Ω load.
