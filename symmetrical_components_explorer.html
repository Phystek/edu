Angle convention

Angle convention: textbooks vary. Glover defines the rotation operator with 0–360° angles (a² = 1∠240°) but often reports worked answers in whichever form is shortest, including negative angles (−120°). Both describe the same phasor; the toggle only changes how angles are displayed.
