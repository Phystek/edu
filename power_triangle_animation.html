Where the Power Triangle Comes From

ELEC2005 · Week 8

Why does an AC load have three different powers, and why do they make a right-angled triangle? Build the load up one part at a time: a resistor, then an inductor, then both, and watch the current, the power waveform and the triangle grow together. Finish by correcting the power factor with a capacitor.

Slowed right down, with ideal parts. One AC cycle here takes 4 seconds; on the 50 Hz mains it takes 20 ms. The load is a pure resistor, inductor and capacitor in parallel across a 230 V (rms) supply, so each branch carries only one kind of power. Numbers on the circuit and the triangle are rms; the waveforms show instantaneous values.
