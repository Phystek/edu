Depth of Discharge and Cycle Life

ELEC2005 · Week 12

A home battery is cycled once a day to supply a fixed amount of energy. Choose how deeply it is discharged each day. A shallow depth of discharge (DoD) makes each cycle gentler, so the battery lasts more cycles, but you must buy a bigger battery to get the same daily energy. Which DoD gives the cheapest energy over the battery’s life?
