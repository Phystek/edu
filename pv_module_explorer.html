PV Cell and Module Explorer

ELEC2005 · Week 11

A 36-cell PV module, modelled with the single-diode equivalent circuit from the lecture. Change the sunlight, the cell temperature and the two parasitic resistances, and watch the i–v and p–v curves, the maximum power point (MPP) and the fill factor respond. The faded curve is the module at Standard Test Conditions, for comparison.

Textbook model, real numbers. Cell parameters are Masters’ 36-cell example (Ch. 8): Isc = 3.4 A at 1000 W/m², I0 = 6×10−10 A, Rp = 6.6 Ω, Rs = 5 mΩ per cell. The temperature effect uses the standard silicon approximation (I0 rises steeply with T), so treat the temperature results as typical rather than exact for any particular product.
