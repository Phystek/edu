A cartoon of the chemistry, with real numbers on the graph. Ions and electrons are drawn far fewer, larger and slower than real ones, the electrodes visibly thin and thicken, and the charge build-up without a salt bridge (which really takes a tiny fraction of a second) is slowed down. The graph and readouts use the Nernst equation, E = 1.10 V − (RT/2F) ln([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺]), for 100 mL of 1 M solution in each half-cell at 25 °C. The internal resistance (5 Ω when fresh) is made to rise as the copper ions run out, a simple stand-in for concentration polarisation.
