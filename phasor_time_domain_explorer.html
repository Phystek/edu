Phasors · Rotating-Vector Representation

The rms phasor V̄ = V∠δ (dashed maroon arrow) is a frozen snapshot, at t = 0, of a vector that's really rotating counter-clockwise at angular speed ω. Press Play to watch that rotating vector (solid teal arrow, peak magnitude √2·V) sweep around — its projection onto the real axis, traced by the moving dot, is exactly v(t). Turn on the current phasor to see the same thing happen for Ī, and to explore how the phase angle φ = δ − β between them shows up as a lag or lead in the time domain.
