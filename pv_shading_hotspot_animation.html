Partial Shading, Hot Spots and Bypass Diodes

ELEC2005 · Week 11

A 36-cell module charging a 12 V battery. Cells in series must all carry the same current, so what happens when one of them is in shade? Watch the current stall, find out why the ideal model gets it wrong, see the shaded cell heat up, and watch bypass diodes switch on to limit the damage. Then shade any cells yourself.

Real model, simplified wiring. Each cell uses Masters’ single-diode parameters (Isc = 3.4 A, Rp = 6.6 Ω, Rs = 5 mΩ), at 25 °C. The battery is an ideal voltage source (12 V unless the story moves it), and each bypass diode is treated as a fixed 0.6 V drop when it conducts. A blocking diode (not drawn) stops the battery pushing current backwards. Photons and moving dots are a picture of the flow, not to scale.
