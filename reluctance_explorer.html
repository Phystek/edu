Magnetic Circuits

Reluctance Explorer

ELEC2005 · Week 10

A core with an air gap can be analysed like a simple electric circuit. The winding's ampere-turns act as the source, and the core and the gap are two reluctances in series. Change the gap, the core material, the turns and the current to see what sets the flux, and notice how quickly even a small gap takes over.

Simplified model. The core is treated as linear (a fixed μr), with no saturation and no fringing at the gap. A real core saturates at roughly 1.5 to 2 T, so a flux density above that is shown in red and is illustrative only.
