A practical switch takes a finite time tc to transition between on and off. During that crossover, voltage and current are non-zero at the same time — the shaded overlap is energy lost as heat. The ideal switch (dashed) has no crossover and no loss. Drag the sliders to see how transition time and switching frequency drive power loss.

Simplified model: linear V/I ramps during the crossover, zero on-state conduction drop, zero off-state leakage, and a fixed 50% on/off duty split. Real devices also lose energy from conduction and leakage — this widget isolates switching loss only.
