Battery Pack Builder: Cells in Series and Parallel

ELEC2005 · Week 12

Every battery, from a car’s 12 V starter battery to an EV pack, is built from cells. Cells in series add their voltages; cells in parallel add their capacities (amp-hours). The stored energy depends only on how many cells you use. Pick a cell, choose how many go in series (S) and in parallel (P), or start from a real-world example.
