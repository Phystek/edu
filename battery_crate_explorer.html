A 12 V lead–acid battery discharged at constant current until it reaches its cut-off voltage. Change the discharge rate and see three things the lecture describes: the voltage sits lower, the cut-off voltage is lower, and the battery runs out sooner and delivers less of its rated capacity. Rated capacity is the 20-hour (C20) figure.

Simplified model. Run time follows Peukert’s law, t = 20 h × (Qrated ⁄ (20 h × I))k, with an adjustable Peukert exponent k. The shape of the voltage curve (and its 0.6 Ω·Ah ⁄ Q effective resistance) is illustrative of a typical lead–acid battery, not a specific product. Peukert’s law is a rule of thumb, and is least accurate at very high rates.
