Balanced Three-Phase Circuits

Y/Δ Circuit & Phasor Explorer

ELEC2005 · Week 9

Set the source voltage and the load impedance, then switch the same three impedances between a Y and a Δ connection. The line-to-line voltage is always √3 times the line-to-neutral voltage and leads it by 30°. For a Y load the line current is the load current; for a Δ load the two differ by the same √3 and 30°.
