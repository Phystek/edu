Power in Balanced Three-Phase Circuits

Three-Phase Instantaneous Power Explorer

ELEC2005 · Week 9

Each single phase delivers a pulsing instantaneous power — a steady term plus a ripple at twice the line frequency. With enough evenly-spaced phases, those ripples cancel exactly and the total power becomes perfectly constant. Adjust the number of phases and their spacing to discover the condition for yourself, or use the unbalance controls to break a working configuration and watch the ripple return.
