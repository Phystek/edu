Power in Balanced Three-Phase Circuits

Three-Phase Instantaneous Power Explorer

ELEC2005 · Week 9

Every phase delivers a pulsing power: a steady part plus a ripple at twice the line frequency. With the right number of phases at the right spacing those ripples cancel and the total becomes perfectly constant. Change the number of phases and their spacing to find the condition, then unbalance phase a and watch the ripple return.

Why it cancels (or doesn't)

Each phase's ripple is a phasor at twice that phase's angle. The black arrow is their average: when it shrinks to nothing, the ripples cancel and the total power is constant.
