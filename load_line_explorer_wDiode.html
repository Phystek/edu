Illustrative diode — not to scale. Its turn-on is deliberately stretched across several volts (instead of a real silicon diode's ~0.6–0.7 V) purely so the whole knee, the load line, and where it crosses each axis all fit on one fixed graph. Real diode behaviour is covered with accurate numbers elsewhere in this lecture.

The diode's own curve and the circuit's load line each describe a valid V–I relationship — only one point (Q) satisfies both. Drag the sliders and watch the load line pivot and slide as R and VS change, and watch Q move along the curve to match.
