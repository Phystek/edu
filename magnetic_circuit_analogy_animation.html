Magnetic Circuits as Electric Circuits

ELEC2005 · Week 10

You already know how to analyse a simple electric circuit. A magnetic circuit, such as a core with a coil and an air gap, works in almost exactly the same way, with different names for each part. Step through the chapters: the matching parts of the two circuits pulse together, and the translation table fills in as you go.

Twins, not the same circuit. The electric circuit on the left is a made-up twin, with values chosen so that every ratio matches the magnetic circuit. The magnetic circuit is linear, with no leakage or fringing (until the last chapter), and the air gap is drawn much wider than it really is so you can see it.
