Finding the Peak: MPPT on a Shaded String

ELEC2005 · Week 11

Three PV modules in series, each with a bypass diode, feeding a dc–dc converter that controls the string voltage. Shade the modules and the p–v curve grows several peaks. Then run a simple perturb-and-observe (P&O) tracker and watch whether it finds the true maximum, or gets stuck on a smaller local peak.

Simplified on purpose. Each module is Masters’ 36-cell example (55 W at 1000 W/m², 25 °C), with every cell in a module equally shaded, and one bypass diode per module (a fixed 0.6 V drop). The tracker is slowed right down: a real converter takes a step every few milliseconds.
