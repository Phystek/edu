Week 4 · BJT as a Switch

BJT Switch Voltage-Transfer-Characteristic Explorer

ELEC2005 · Wk 4

Sweep the input voltage VIN from 0 up to VCC and watch the transistor step through all three regions: cutoff (off — output pulled high), active (the brief transition), and saturation (on — output pulled low). RB and RC reshape where that transition happens and how sharp it is — this is exactly the design trade-off behind choosing switch resistor values.
